A Saint Lucia travel companion

Every island has its secrets. Ours has a guide.

Hélène of the West is a Saint Lucian company that curates the island for you — the beaches worth the drive, the rainforest trails worth the mud, the food worth the queue.

We pair every experience with a vetted local guide or driver, and we handle the part of island travel that usually goes wrong: getting there and getting back. Private taxis and airport transfers are booked directly through this site, at fixed fares, with drivers we know by name.

Whether you land tomorrow or live here and want to see your island again, we plan it, book it and confirm it in minutes.
