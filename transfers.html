Transfers & taxi

Landed. Collected. Settled.

Private airport transfers and island taxi service with verified local drivers, fixed fares agreed before you travel, and a name board waiting at arrivals.

Book a Transfer

The ninety minutes that set the tone

Most visitors land at Hewanorra in the south and stay in the north — a beautiful ninety-minute drive over the island’s spine, and a stressful one to arrange at a taxi rank after a long flight. We remove the uncertainty entirely.

Your driver is licensed, vetted and tracking your flight before you land. Delayed? They wait, and the fare doesn’t change. The vehicle is air-conditioned, the water in the cooler is cold, and the commentary on the way is optional — some guests want the island introduced, some want quiet. Both are respected.

The same drivers run our island taxi service: point-to-point rides, restaurant runs, and full-day hires routed however you like.

Fare guide

Fixed fares, no negotiation

Quoted per vehicle for up to six guests, confirmed at booking. Routes not listed? Every corner of the island is covered — ask when you book.

How it works

Three steps, no surprises

  1. Book with your flight number

    Tell us your route, date and flight. You’ll receive instant confirmation with your driver’s name and vehicle.

  2. We track your arrival

    Your driver monitors the flight and adjusts for delays automatically. No charge, no anxious messages from the tarmac.

  3. Meet at the name board

    Step out of arrivals, find your name, hand over the bags. The fare was fixed when you booked — it stays fixed.
